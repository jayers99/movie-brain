# Tier ranker contract (moved from CLAUDE.md 2026-09-21)

- **Tier ranker** (spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-13-tier-ranker-design.md`, migration 022): `/rank` places owned films into five tiers by binary search against one anchor film per tier (`domain/rank.py::next_step`, nine legal verdict sequences, `better`/`worse` only — no `same`); seeded from `my_ratings` (`tier_for_score`: 10→1, 9→2, 8→3, 7→4, 6→5, below 6 raises (never seeded)) which it NEVER writes; tables `rank_session` (one open per source, `last_action` = one-level undo), `rank_anchor`, `rank_placement` (the result; `how` ∈ seed/compared/anchor), `rank_comparison` (append-only log, never read to compute a tier), `rank_deferral`, and `unseen` (durable pass bucket on the watchlist pattern — `set_unseen` and the drawer's toggle are the only writers; marking a film unseen deletes its `rank_placement` AND `rank_comparison` rows in every session, since an unseen film's old clicks must audit nothing). The queue is derived per request (pool − placed − unseen − anchors, seeded shuffle by `queue_key`, films mid-search first, deferrals last) and never stored; the queue, the anchor proposal's fallback and `swap_anchor` all exclude disposed films too. A tier whose anchor is NULL or has itself gone unseen blocks the next pair (`needs_anchor`, derived on every read — D10's slot-emptying holds whichever way the film lost its seat); `start_session` answers a session already open with an explicit 409 check rather than a raw `sqlite3` exception (the application layer imports no `sqlite3`); and a crash between logging a verdict and placing the film self-heals on the next read instead of asserting. Five body-taking `/api/rank/*` routes share `web/app.py::_json_object()` for their one 400 shape check; `rank.js` serialises every click and keydown through one promise queue so a fast second click can't read a stale pair; the page's notes render in `#note` in the header, always visible even before a session exists. The drawer's Unseen toggle is its own row directly below the `<h2>` — never inside it, so it can't perturb the pinned title — class `unseen-toggle`, `aria-pressed` set from the server's answer. Save writes `film_list` slug `my-owned-tiers` (`RANKER_SLUGS`, `curator = 'me'`, tied `rank_label`s per tier, trust untouched) through `replace_list_entries`; `lists import` refuses a ranker slug and the save refuses a slug with a `lists/<slug>.tsv`. `merge_film` moves the five film-scoped tables survivor-wins (`rank_session` is per source, not per film, and needs no merge handling). **Order tier 1** (spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-13-order-top-tier-design.md`, migration 023): a second tab on `/rank` (hash `#order`) strict-orders the LIVE session's tier 1 by binary insertion — `domain/rank.py::order_step(order, verdicts)` derives the slot bounds from the candidate's logged `(other_film_id, verdict)` pairs on every read, never from stored lo/hi (`better` caps the slot at that film's index, `worse` floors it at index + 1; a crossed bound is a corrupt log, raised in the domain and reported as `corrupt` by the use case, never a 500). Tables `rank_order` (dense 1..k positions per session + tier, shifted one row at a time because of the UNIQUE), `rank_order_comparison` (append-only, the search's only state) and `rank_order_deferral` (order-mode Pass, separate from the tiering's). `ORDER_TIERS` in `domain/rank.py` are the tiers exposed — `(1, 2)` at first, every tier `(1, 2, 3, 4, 5)` since 2026-09-15. The first film into an empty order is inserted with no click on the next read. `set_unseen` purges a departing film from the order AND every verdict naming it on either side (`_purge_order_rows`); `merge_film` deletes every verdict naming the LOSER on the other side for the same reason (a re-point would move the verdict's index) while moving the loser's own candidate log survivor-wins per session — that is what keeps derived bounds consistent. One undo slot for the page: `last_action` carries `mode`. Save writes each ORDERED tier's films as bare lines followed by that tier's unordered rest tied at the first unordered line (a lone one bare); tiers without an order stay tied as before. No `Have not seen` in order mode; `1`/`2` are inert there. Routes `GET /api/rank/order`, `POST /api/rank/order/verdict`, `POST /api/rank/order/pass`; a missing session is 404 like every other rank route. `merge_film` is survivor-wins per session on the candidate column and delete-everything on the other column of `rank_order_comparison`, so a candidate judged against a merged-away film simply re-probes. Undoing a tier 1 placement in tiers mode also drops the film from `rank_order`. **Ranking pool** (spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-13-ranking-pool-design.md`, migration 024): the ranker no longer asks only `owned` — `Repository.rank_pool_film_ids()` is the ONE method defining who the ranker asks and seeds (owned ∪ `rank_mark` ∪ rated 6–10, minus disposed, unseen, and any film scored 0–5), and `pool_seed_films()` (pool ∩ rated 6–10) drives seeding, which now runs on every read (`application/rank.py::_seed_new`, called first in both `session_state` and `order_state`) rather than only at session start; the seed map's bottom moved — `tier_for_score` maps 6→5 and a score below 6 raises (`ValueError`), never seeding a tier at all; a film that leaves the pool (rated 0–5, or unmarked after placement) is never asked again but keeps its placement, order position, any anchor seat and its saved-list line until it is marked unseen — Phase B decides whether leaving the pool should cascade like unseen does; `rank_mark` (migration 024, "Rank this" — the pool's entry ticket for a film the owner does not own) is written ONLY by the drawer's toggle and `set_rank_mark`, and lives in `_ONE_ROW_TABLES` for `merge_film`'s survivor-wins sweep; `domain/rank.py::ORDER_TIERS` is the tiers the order mode exposes (`(1, 2)` then, `(1, 2, 3, 4, 5)` since 2026-09-15 — the owner asked to order every tier; nothing else changed because every order table, route, undo action and the save were already keyed by `tier`), threaded as `tier` through all three order routes (`GET /api/rank/order`, `POST /api/rank/order/verdict`, `POST /api/rank/order/pass`) and the page's `#order-1` … `#order-5` hashes (bare legacy `#order` still means tier 1; the tab bar is `Tiers` plus an `Order tier` group of five numbered tabs, and `rank.js` mirrors `ORDER_TIERS` to parse the hash); `undo` falls back to tier 1 when a stored `last_action` carries no `tier` (rows written before tier 2 existed); `start_session`/`swap_anchor` accept any pool film as an anchor, not only owned ones; `owned_seed_films` no longer exists; `DEFAULT_LIST_NAME["owned"]` is now `"My Ranking"` (renamed from "My films, tiered" and then "My Ranked", both 2026-09-14) (the `my-owned-tiers` slug is unchanged); and the ranker still never writes `my_ratings` — tiering's D2 holds until Phase B (ratings follow the ranking) deliberately reverses it. **Move a film to another tier** (spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-14-move-tier-design.md`, migration 025 widens `rank_placement.how` to `seed/compared/anchor/moved` by table rebuild): the drawer's Tier row (`button.tier-pick`, rendered only when the detail-only `rank_tier` is non-null) POSTs `/api/rank/move {film_id, tier}` → `application/rank.py::move_film`, which REPLACES the placement row with `how='moved'` (never delete-then-insert — `_seed_new` would re-seed it) and purges the film from the open session's order — order row, every `rank_order_comparison` naming it on either side, its order deferral (`_purge_order_rows` with the new optional `session_id`; the unseen purge stays cross-session) — because the candidate log has no tier filter and deferrals no tier column; the film then waits UNORDERED in the new tier for the Order tab (no edge placement), `rank_status` reports `awaiting_order` (detail keys `rank_tier`/`rank_how`/`awaiting_order`, never on `FilmView`, never seeding), and the drawer's "Rank this" is pressed AND disabled while awaiting (one signal: the ranker still owes the film a pass). Refused 409 for an unplaced film, an anchor (`swap the anchor first`) or the same tier; a move clears `last_action` (a stale undo of a placing verdict would unplace it). `rank.js` now refreshes after a FAILED verdict/pass/undo too, so a pair whose candidate was moved from another tab is replaced instead of re-409ing forever. Tiering verdicts are left alone; `merge_film`/`set_unseen`/`save_list` needed no change. **Re-rank (M12/M13, same afternoon):** on a PLACED film, "Rank this" means "this ranking is wrong, ask me again" — `POST /api/rank/rerank {film_id}` → `rerank_film` deletes the placement, the film's own tiering verdicts and deferral, purges its order rows (session-scoped) and SETS the mark; `_seed_new` skips marked films (so a rated film is asked by comparison, never re-seeded) and then runs `clear_served_marks` — a mark comes off once the film is placed and ordered in its tier (every tier is orderable since 2026-09-15), so the mark is a pending request, never a label (a non-owned unrated film leaves the pool on service and keeps its rows per pool §4.6). The drawer button has four states: unplaced+unmarked = pool ticket, unplaced+marked = lit (click un-marks), placed+awaiting = lit+disabled, placed+ordered = unlit (click re-ranks, `data-placed`).
