10. [x] **Curated top-N lists** — import named ranked lists (top 100 / top N) and show in the drawer, per list, whether the film is on it and at what rank. Data model on the external-authority pattern: a `lists` registry (slug PK, name, source URL, size, ordered or unordered) plus `list_entries` (list_slug + film_id + rank + raw title/year as listed), matched through the shared `match_candidates` core (never a new matcher); unmatched or ambiguous entries queue to `match_review` under the list's authority so the human pass resolves them, and entries with no film in the DB become discovery films via the Mode-B promotion path. Drawer shows a "Lists" row of `<list name> #<rank>` badges; later, a filter chip / sort per list. First list to build: **Cahiers du Cinéma's 100 Ideal Cinematheque Films** (Cahiers' 2008 "100 films pour une cinémathèque idéale"). — shipped across four increments, 2026-08-28/29. **Three lists live and 100% linked:** `cahiers-100` (100), `bergan-100` (100), `sight-and-sound-2022` (264). The mechanism: checked-in `lists/<slug>.tsv` files; `lists import` links and asks but **never creates a film**; `lists create` is the one creating path, behind three gates plus gate 2b (`find_by_imdb` when the winning candidate is OMDb-only); `list` review rows drain with `--film`/`--create`/`--dismiss` only, since a list entry has no film to key. Then three increments the first list did not need — **supplied IMDb ids** (migration 014): a source carrying ids does not skip the resolver, it runs anyway and the verdict is *compared*, which makes each such import a labelled test of the resolver (on Bergan it agreed 98/100 and disagreed 0); **tied ranks** (015): `film_list_entry.rank` is the entry's position and `rank_label` holds what the poll printed, without which no critics' poll was importable at all — Sight & Sound ties 233 of its 264; **trust and the tally** (016): `film_list.trust` (default 1, so the weighted tally starts identical to a raw count), an `N lists` badge on the card, and an `on 2+ lists` chip. **23 films sit on all three lists, 70 on two — the chip surfaces 93**, of which 30 are owned and 40 are on Criterion now. Still open from the original description: per-list filter and rank-order sort (item 11).
