[x] **Scattered-failure guard on the weekly provider refresh** *(fixed 2026-09-20: both inferred consequences reproduced by failing tests first — the failed film dropped off its services AND its next good check fired a false arrival; fix = a failed lookup carries the film's current TMDB-fed listings forward with no transition, `Repository.carry_listings_forward`, rather than any of the three stamp-withholding candidates below, because withholding re-fetches the whole catalogue on the next sync and a permanently failing film (a TMDB 404 is a `RequestException` here) would hold the stamp back for ever, which keeps genuinely dropped services reading current. The live DB showed no film bitten: 0 of 4,947 keyed films checked before the stamp, and the 16 reappearance events on record are single-service rotations. Named at the time and fixed the same day: a TMDB 404 on the providers call was counted as weather, so a film TMDB had deleted would have been carried forward for ever — a 404 is now an answer, "nowhere to watch", stamped and logged)* — found 2026-09-01 while reading Radarr for borrowable algorithms. Radarr gates every destructive cleanup on `if (!listItemsResult.AnyFailure)` — *any* failed list fetch, not a run of them — because acting on a partial fetch looks exactly like the world having changed. movie-brain has the coarse version of that guard already: `tmdb_step` withholds the `tmdb_providers_refreshed_at` stamp whenever a pass aborts (`application/availability.py`). The gap is that a pass only aborts after `MAX_CONSECUTIVE_FAILURES` **in a row**, and `_refresh_pass` resets `consecutive = 0` on every success, so a scattered error rate never trips it: the stamp advances, and the films whose TMDB call quietly failed keep a `last_seen` behind the new stamp. Since "current" for TMDB-fed sources is `last_seen >= tmdb_providers_refreshed_at`, those films read as no-longer-current — they drop out of the default `reachable` scope, and their next successful refresh writes an `availability_transitions` row against a frontier that says they were absent, i.e. a false "New on:" arrival for a film that never left. **Verified:** the consecutive-counter reset, and that scattered failures cannot abort a pass. **Inferred, not verified:** the false-arrival consequence — write the failing test first. Candidate fixes, cheapest first: count failures per pass rather than consecutively and withhold the stamp above a threshold; or track the failed film ids and exclude them from the currency comparison; or (Radarr's own answer, strictest) withhold the stamp on any failure at all. Two further Radarr borrowings came out of the same read and are deliberately NOT filed here, being design work rather than a defect: typed rejection-reason enums for the thumbprint resolver (whose free-text `reason` strings are also the benchmark contract), and Radarr's split between what matched and what the match is worth (`ProfileFormatItem.Score`, summed per profile), which is the shape the per-title override in `observations/presentation.tsv` eventually wants.
