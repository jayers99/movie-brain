# docs/raw — the film-craft source corpus

Immutable source texts for the MovieBrain film LLM-wiki. **Read, never edit** — same three-layer discipline as the Halo vault: `docs/raw/` is source, `docs/wiki/` is the synthesis you build from it, and citations point back here by filename.

## What's here (pulled 2026-09-01)

All files below are **free, DRM-free PDFs released by the authors** on davidbordwell.net — Bordwell and Thompson made their previously-paid online books free on 2022-05-17 and describe them as "unlocked pdf files."

| File | Title | Notes |

| `on-history-film-style-davidbordwell-180531.pdf` | **On the History of Film Style**, 2nd ed. | 28 MB. The core text for staging and visual style — the single most relevant book in the corpus |

## Not obtained, and why

- **Christopher Nolan: A Labyrinth of Linkages** — the 2022 announcement lists it as free, but no PDF link survives on `nolan.php`, the books index, or the blog sidebar. Ask the site, or re-check later.
- **Ozu and the Poetics of Cinema** — hosted at `quod.lib.umich.edu` as an online HTML book, not a single PDF. Needs a scrape, not a download.
- **Figures Traced in Light** (UC Press) — the staging book, print only, no clean digital route found. **Buy in print.**
- **Film Art: An Introduction** (McGraw-Hill) — ebook is Connect/VitalSource, DRM'd, not ingestible.
- **Gulino, *Screenwriting: The Sequence Approach*** (Bloomsbury) and **Mackendrick, *On Film-making*** (Faber) — ebooks exist on Kindle and Google Play only, both DRM'd. **Held** until the free corpus is indexed and the gaps are known.

## The bigger free source, not yet pulled

**davidbordwell.net/blog — *Observations on Film Art*, ~20 years of long-form, film-by-film style analysis with named examples.** For this project's purpose (concept → reference-standard film) that blog is probably a better index than any single textbook, and it is free and scrapeable. It is the obvious next ingest.
