# Film Art: An Introduction — table of contents

**Source of truth for the film-craft curriculum ordering.** A table of contents is metadata, not content — the book's text is DRM-locked and unreadable, but its chapter sequence is freely available and is a teaching order refined across a dozen editions of the most widely used film textbook there is.

## Why Part Three is the curriculum

**Chapters 4 through 7 are the four craft channels**, and they map almost exactly onto the six-branch flavour wheel: mise-en-scène (staging, blocking, setting, lighting) · cinematography (framing, lens, exposure, camera movement) · editing · sound. **Chapter 8, "Style as a Formal System," is the one that matters most at the end** — it is the chapter that teaches how the channels combine into a whole, which is what a canon judgment actually rests on.

Chapters 1–3 are conceptual groundwork. Chapters 9–12 are types, criticism and history — worth reaching, but not where an untrained eye should start.
