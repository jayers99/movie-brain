"Wishlist it" — the fallback, two ways

The button cannot add the film for you silently, so it does the next best thing: it works out the target, copies it to your clipboard, and opens the film's CheapCharts page. You press the star there and paste. The only open question is when the heart appears.

This preview is real where it can be. Clicking the button really copies the real target price and really opens the real CheapCharts page in a new tab — so you can try the whole motion, paste included. No price is ever shown on this page: that is your rule. One row is rigged to fail, to show you the wording; it is marked pretend.

A · The heart appears at once Recommended

One click, heart on the row, done — exactly the motion you described. If you did not finish the add on CheapCharts, click ♥ Wishlisted in the drawer to take the heart off again. Recommended because you said simplicity wins.

B · It asks you first

Same click. When you come back, the drawer asks "Did you add it?" The heart appears only when you say yes. The heart is never wrong, but every film costs one more click.
