"Wishlist it" — three mock-ups of your design

Your design, as I understood it: a small wishlisted mark on the film's row, after the list count — nothing at all on other rows. In the drawer of a film that is not wishlisted, one button. Click it, and the film goes onto your CheapCharts wishlist at one dollar above its lowest price ever, and the mark appears on the row.

These are clickable. Click any row to open that film; click the button and watch the row. The looks are copied from your real dashboard. All prices are real, read from CheapCharts today.

Two honest limits. The click here is pretend — nothing is sent anywhere. And no row starts out wishlisted, because I cannot see your real CheapCharts wishlist yet.
