What may surprise you

  • "White" only works while the drawer is open. Your page is already white; the drawer dims it to grey, and the open row is the one thing left un-dimmed. After you close the drawer nothing is dimmed, so a white row would be invisible — variants B and C mark it with a grey fill and a dark bar at the left edge instead. That look is my guess, not your choice; say so if it is wrong.
  • The white row is not clickable. As today, a click anywhere outside the drawer closes it — the white row included. The list still cannot be scrolled while the drawer is open (except in C, where ↑ ↓ scroll it for you).
  • The mark is a bookmark, not a filter. Clear leaves it alone. It is forgotten when you reload the page, and it is not part of the address you can copy.
  • On a narrow window there is nothing to see. The drawer is 760 pixels wide. If your browser window is not much wider than that, the drawer covers the row. Nothing changes for that case.
  • Opening a film from a link or the Back button does not scroll the list to it. If the row happens to be on screen it is white; if not, the list stays where it was.
  • Only in C: ↑ ↓ do nothing while you are typing in a rating or a note, and they stop at the first and last film of what the list is showing.
