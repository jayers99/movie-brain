**Outcome.** Editing while you browse never leaves you nowhere. The list behind the drawer always shows where you are, and the film under your hands is always the next one to decide.

**What wins when things trade off.** As ↓ would have done. The move is exactly a step: the same white row, the same nudge of the list, the same one Back to close. Nothing else on the page changes.

**Why it is one rule, not one per chip.** Under Shop a wishlist drops the film; under Unrated a rating does; under Watchlist a star does; under Rewatch a rating does; under Rated by me clearing a rating does; a column filter or a search can drop a film on any of those edits too. The dashboard already answers one question on every redraw — is the open film still in the list? — and already remembers where it was (that is how ↓ carries on from the gap today). The feature is: when a drawer edit is what made the answer "no", take the step at once instead of waiting for ↓.

**What deliberately does not ship.** No move when a chip, the search box, the list picker, a column filter or the Back button changes the list (story 5) — those leave the gap, as today. No move for a film that was never in the list (story 7). No move when the list runs out (story 8). No trail of undone edits: only the last edit has an Undo, and only in the drawer it moved to (story 9). No change to what ↑ ↓ mean. No toast, no animation, no sound. No Undo for an edit that landed in a drawer you had already stepped away from (the film leaves quietly, as today).
