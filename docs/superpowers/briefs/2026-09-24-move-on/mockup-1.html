What may surprise you

  • The drawer moves when the edit lands, not when you press. A star or a rating lands at once. A wishlist click talks to CheapCharts first — several seconds on the real thing (an estimate, never timed), one pretend second here — and the drawer moves on when the answer comes. If you have already pressed ↓ by then, nothing moves: the film now open is still in the list.
  • A chip cannot be pressed while the drawer is open. The chips, the search box, the list picker and the column filters all sit under the grey; a click there closes the drawer, as today. So the only thing that can ever move the drawer is an edit inside it (story 5).
  • When the list runs out, the drawer stays. Un-star the only film left and there is nowhere to move: the drawer stays on it, nothing is white, ↑ ↓ are quiet (story 8). It does not close by itself.
  • Only in B: the undo line remembers the last edit only, and only in the drawer it moved to (story 9). Step, click another row, close, or reload, and the line is gone; the way back is then the chip-off route (A). Undo lands like the edit did: the row comes back at once, and the drawer goes back to the film only if you have not stepped away or closed since pressing Undo — otherwise nothing moves. A failed Undo keeps the line's words and offers Try again.
  • Only in C: one ↑ reaches the film that left, although the hint beside ✕ still says "previous film". From that film, ↑ goes to the film before the gap and ↓ to the film that took its place, as today.
  • Nothing here is sent anywhere. The wishlist click is pretend (one second, no CheapCharts); the star and the rating change only this page.
