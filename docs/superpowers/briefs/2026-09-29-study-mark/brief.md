**Outcome.** A viewing can carry one mark: *study* — there is a technique here I must watch for a second time. It is set from the sentence that names the reason, or on a past line by its number; it shows on the line in the drawer; the Watched chip's second state lists what is waiting; nothing clears it but you.

**What wins when things trade off.** The mark is on the viewing, never the film (the log's rule: everything hangs off the viewing); "the film is marked" is a read — any of its lines marked. It never moves a rating, a tier, a rank or the watchlist. It is one word, and it is not "rewatch": that chip already means something else.

**What deliberately does not ship.** No button in the drawer — the word is not one (story 6). No automatic clearing when a second viewing is logged (story 7). No second tag or free text — one mark, one meaning (story 12). No badge in the film's row and no column (story 5). No rename of the Rewatch chip (story 10). No inference from praise (story 3). No refresh of the whole list on focus: a line logged with the drawer closed reaches the list on the next reload or drawer open (stories 2 and 4). No warning when a refresh of the open film fails after a successful command (a server hiccup): the drawer keeps what it showed and the next focus tries again — a warning there would fire on every alt-tab while the server was down. No second tag (story 12); no analysis loop (story 13). No effect on ranking, tiers or the notes loop — backlog 5 is where the second watch's analysis will land.
