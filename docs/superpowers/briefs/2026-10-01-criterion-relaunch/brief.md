# Criterion relaunch — brief 1.1

Backlog 50. Design: `../../specs/2026-10-01-criterion-relaunch-design.md` (decisions D1–D13). Discovery: `../../specs/2026-10-01-criterion-relaunch-seed.md`. Nothing on screen changes, so there is no mock-up: every story is something the owner runs at the terminal or sees on the existing dashboard. Films named are real films in the live database on 2026-10-01; every count re-derives from the saved captures in `../../research/2026-10-01-criterion-relaunch/`. 1.1 answers gap check A (`trial-log.md`).

## Stories

1. **Sync works again.** I run `movie-brain sync`. It no longer stops at "no window.TOKEN"; the Criterion part finishes and prints one line: `criterion — arrived N · left N · to review N`.
2. **The bridge shows me what drifted before it writes.** I run `movie-brain criterion bridge`. About 25–30 minutes later (one catalog walk, then one quick check per old link) it tells me how many old links led to a film, how many are gone, how many became extras, how many clash, and lists the films whose title or year Criterion changed — 97 by year, 72 by title, 9 both on October 1 (Test Pattern 2019 → 2021, Riotsville, U.S.A. → Riotsville, USA). Nothing is written until I add `--apply`.
3. **A film Criterion re-dated stays my film.** Nadja (#3057) is rated 5 and Criterion now calls it 1995. After the bridge and a sync it is still #3057, still 1994 in my catalogue, still rated 5, still current on Criterion, and it is not counted as a new arrival.
4. **A film I already hold that Criterion just added joins the film I have.** Barry Lyndon (#3433) was in my catalogue from Apple TV and TCM; Criterion added it on October 1. After the sync it is the same #3433, now also on Criterion, and it shows as new on Criterion from the day I run that sync (movie-brain dates an arrival by when it saw it, not by Criterion's October 1) — no second Barry Lyndon.
5. **A film on my watchlist that left shows as departed.** Some Came Running (#33) left Criterion on September 30. After the sync it is no longer current on Criterion; it is not deleted, and its other services stay.
6. **The Leaving chip shows October's list.** After the sync the Leaving chip holds the 28 films leaving October 31 (Zabriskie Point, Eve's Bayou among them), each reading "Leaving October 31". The 39 "September 30" labels are gone.
7. **A film the resolver cannot place waits for me, it is not invented.** A new Criterion film the resolver cannot match, or one JW Player says it has no record of, becomes a review row in `movie-brain review list --authority criterion`, one row per film. I settle it with `review resolve ID --film X` (it is that film), `--create` (a new film under the title, year and director the row shows — refused, naming the film, if I already hold one that matches) or `--dismiss` (never ask again — the sync stops looking it up). On the next sync a linked or created one is listed on Criterion.
8. **A broken night changes nothing.** If the catalog or one new film's JW Player lookup fails halfway (the network, not a "no such film" answer), sync says so, exits 1, and my catalogue is exactly as it was — no half-written arrivals. The rest of the night's sync still runs — ratings, the other services, the enrichment chain — so only Criterion waits for the next night.
9. **Directors fill in where I see none.** Of the 124 Criterion films without a stored director, 117 already show OMDb's and stay exactly as they are. Of the 7 that show none, the 3 still on Criterion (Keeping Secrets Will Destroy You #735, The Horse in Focus #1766, The IX Olympiad in Amsterdam #1777) get Criterion's; the other 4 have left. A director I can already see never changes.
10. **"Watch on Criterion" opens the film.** In the drawer, the Criterion link opens the film's page on the new site (`/films/<id>/<slug>`; for a newly added film `/films/<id>`, which the site forwards to the same page).
11. **A film that left loses its Leaving label the night it leaves.** Some Came Running, labelled "September 30", is no longer labelled once the sync sees it gone — even on a night the leaving pages could not be read.

## Deliberately not shipping (stories the owner might bump into)

12. **Criterion's editorial shelves are not a chip.** I will not see "October Newly Added" or "Popular Now" in movie-brain; new-on-Criterion is still worked out from what arrived in the catalogue.
13. **Series stay out.** The 33 Criterion series (e.g. Blossoms Shanghai) do not appear as films.
14. **An early exit Criterion never announces is not caught until it leaves.** A film Criterion pulls without putting it on a leaving page shows no "Leaving" label; it just departs on the next sync.
15. **Films Criterion turned into extras disappear.** Contras' City (#351) is still on Criterion, but only as an extra on another film. After the sync it is departed and, since I never rated or watchlisted it, I no longer see it; movie-brain tracks feature films only.
16. **`sync --full` is the same as `sync`.** I type it out of habit; every sync now reads the whole catalogue, and the "full walk" line is gone.
17. **Criterion's new spellings do not rename my films.** Criterion now prints "Riotsville, USA"; my dashboard keeps "Riotsville, U.S.A." (#130); Criterion's spelling is kept only in the record of what Criterion printed.
18. **Series of films Criterion now sells as one "series" leave my list.** My six Lone Wolf and Cub films (#1720, #2067–2071), the five Agnès de ci de là Varda episodes and the four Four Journeys into Mystic Time films all lead to one Criterion series item each. movie-brain tracks films, not series, so all 15 show departed from Criterion after the sync (I have rated, watchlisted or watched none of them); nothing is merged or deleted.
