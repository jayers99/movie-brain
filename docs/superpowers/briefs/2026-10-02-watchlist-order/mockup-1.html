What you should know before saying yes

  • Which variant (A or B) is the one open choice on this page.
  • I made one call you did not: when Watchlist is on together with On a list or Watched, today those two chips put their own order first (list score, last watched). I recommend your order wins whenever Watchlist is on, so the arrows never disappear because of a chip (story 5). The alternative is that those two chips keep their order and the arrows hide. Say "flip 5" if you want the alternative.
  • Where the arrows do hide: a column sort (story 6), a picked list from the list menu (story 11), and a word search in the bar that ranks its answers (story 12) — in each of those the list is not in your order, so an arrow could not mean "one place up". A Title column filter or a director: search only narrows, so the arrows stay.
  • Undo puts a film back in its place, not at the top (story 7), and so does a star taken off and put back in the same drawer (story 13). Only a fresh star goes to the top.
  • A move steps past ONE film you can see; every other film keeps its order (story 4). I changed this from a swap after the gap check: with a swap, the other film could jump over hidden ones without you touching it.
  • Your order is saved at every move and survives a reload. A move that fails to save puts the row back and says so (story 14). In variant B, presses still waiting when you click elsewhere land first; a reload with presses waiting loses them.
  • The hidden ninth film (Some Came Running) keeps a place in the order you cannot see; a swap skips it, exactly like a film a chip hides (story 4).
  • Not in this build: My Ranking arrows, move buttons or keys inside the drawer, drag-and-drop, a "to top" button.
