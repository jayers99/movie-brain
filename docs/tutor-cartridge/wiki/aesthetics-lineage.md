# Aesthetics for a Tutor-Builder — raw/150

**"The Cybernetics of Taste: An Aesthetic Framework for Domain-Specific Tutoring Agents."** NotebookLM deep research, extracted 2026-09-11, 67 sources, ~6.3k words. Commissioned for the tutor build, and read here for one thing only: which positions in the lineage oblige a taste-tutor to behave a particular way.

## What it supplies

Aristotle's *hexis* is the spine. Taste is an **active condition** held ready to perceive — not passive habit — and it arrives through habituation rather than rule-instruction, so a tutor runs repetitions and forced comparisons and audits the state the learner arrives in, and never issues a rubric. The vault already spends hexis on virtue (virtue and the golden mean); the report's usable claim is that the same word does the same work one layer over, making taste a virtue of perception that inherits virtue's training shape. Plato's *mousike* puts sensory calibration before critical vocabulary: exposure first, theory second.

Hume is **where the lineage first concedes the judge can be wrong**, and names the grounds — strong sense, delicacy, practice, comparison, freedom from prejudice. Those five are checkable, so a tutor audits the judging rather than the verdict, which is the move the vault's grading already leans on (canon admission criterion, [[film-canon-epistemology]]). Kant blocks the opposite error: no concept yields a judgment of taste, so nobody can be argued into finding a thing beautiful — the tutor asks how a claim would be defended, never whether it is correct. Sibley's non-condition-governance kills formula-checking ("rule of thirds applied, therefore balanced") and sends the tutor after the passage from physical features to emergent qualities. Bourdieu supplies the bias audit: flag status-marking vocabulary and ask whether a judgment is perception or class reproduction.

Empirical aesthetics closes the loop — Berlyne's inverted-U, processing fluency, Leder's five stages, Vessel's default-mode-network finding — each graded robust or emerging. Carried alongside: a listening-ordered reading path (Sachs's *Ethics* first, Kant via commentary) and three n=1 self-experiments.
