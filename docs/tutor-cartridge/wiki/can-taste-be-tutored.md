# Can Taste Be Tutored — raw/149

**"Computational and Cognitive Architectures for the Scaffolding of Aesthetic Taste."** NotebookLM deep research, extracted 2026-09-11, 66 sources, ~6k words. Commissioned to test the three-layer position held in [[taste]] against the literature, with the tutor as the intended consumer.

## What it supplies

A verdict per layer, in the report's own ordering. **Discrimination: highly trainable, and fast** — perceptual-learning modules brought novices to expert-level classification in 30–90 minutes in ECG reading, dermatology and aviation instruments, with gains intact at six to twelve months; retention held only where feedback carried spatial location, not just correctness. **Defense: conditionally trainable, with real risk of harm.** Verbal overshadowing (Schooler) bites hardest at the intermediate profile — high perceptual expertise, thin domain vocabulary — which is precisely his position in cinema, where analytic verbalising overwrites the high-resolution visual trace. Crossmodal metaphor, his spirits-tasting sheet turned on a frame, is the named workaround. **Disposition: trainable indirectly, never by rule.** Preference does move: mere exposure carries only simple stimuli, while complex work shifts Berlyne's inverted-U optimum rightward through active decoding, and Kant's reflective judgment can be awakened by curated examples but never derived from a concept. Two further layers: tutor-induced distortion, where RLHF sycophancy is worst in multi-turn follow-up and when the learner's opinion sits last in the prompt; and metrics — forced-choice d′, predict-before-reveal calibration error, computed style distance between his photographs and the canon — plus three n=1 protocols.

## Where it landed

The report backs [[taste]] on the three layers and on the disposition resisting direct teaching, and contradicts it twice. First on ordering: discrimination is not merely "the trainable part" but the fastest by an order of magnitude, while the defended layer is the fragile one — a direct warning to the Socratic habit in [[film-study-protocol]]. Second on stability: the disposition's complexity optimum is expected to shift with expertise, so stability of ranking is the wrong test for layer one.
