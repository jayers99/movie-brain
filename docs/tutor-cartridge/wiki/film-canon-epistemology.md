# Film Canon Epistemology — raw/137

**"Epistemology of the Film Canon: An Evaluative Instrument for Aesthetic and Psychometric Calibration."** NotebookLM deep research, submitted 2026-09-01, 96 sources, ~6.4k words, 75 works cited. Audio overview heard on the commute and rated excellent. Declared `rigor: core` — the vault's admission criterion rests on it, and an error here would propagate silently into every canon verdict.

## What it was asked

Whether any defensible weighting across craft dimensions is possible, given MovieWise's 30/30/15/10/10/5 split and its 92/100 canon threshold. The answer is a reasoned refusal, plus a replacement instrument.

## The argument, in four moves

- **Neoformalism (Bordwell/Thompson).** Film form is relational and systemic. A filmmaker may suppress one channel to amplify another, and a narrative transition can be solved by a cut, a sound bridge, or a body moving through the frame. A fixed transhistorical weight cannot describe functionally equivalent devices.
- **Sibley.** Aesthetic properties are not condition-governed by non-aesthetic craft features. Pale colours and curved lines do not entail *delicate*; they equally permit *insipid*. So no arithmetic maps descriptive craft onto an evaluative score.
- **Sadler.** Relative standards resist codification. Rubric language is semantically elastic and its terms mean nothing until instantiated through exemplars. Jonsson & Svingby's review of 75 studies adds that high inter-rater reliability usually reflects raters trained onto countable surface features, not depth.
- **Kalyuga's expertise-reversal effect.** A rigid pre-weighted checklist is a novice scaffold. For an advanced observer it adds extraneous load, and pre-exposure induces anchoring, criterion contamination, and construct-irrelevant narrowing.

Canon formation is then reframed sociologically — Guillory and Bourdieu on cultural capital, citation networks, Bordwell on canonicity as extraordinary deviation from stylistic norms — so a numeric cut-score misunderstands what a canon is.

## The proposed instrument, and what the vault took

TECL, the Teleological Emergent Calibration Ledger (working name **Tickle**). Five phases: sealed free recall · teleological grounding · a single emergent craft channel · Beardsley's complexity/intensity/unity · pairwise calibration against prior films.

Roughly three of the five were already independently built in [[film-study-protocol]] and cinema education, and comparative heats borrowed from spirits judging are a stronger comparative machine than TECL's linear N-vs-N−1. **The two net-new contributions are teleological grounding** (*what problem was this filmmaker solving?*) **and the Beardsley triad**, which supplies the evaluative vocabulary the [[cinema-flavor-wheel]]'s descriptive vocabulary cannot. Unity in particular is the likeliest first casualty of his conception/screenplay/structure gate.
