# Film Craft Taxonomy — raw/138

**"Cognitive and Empirical Taxonomy of Film Craft."** NotebookLM deep research, 2026-09-01, 83 sources, ~4.7k words. Commissioned as a vocabulary *tree*, explicitly not a rubric — the descriptive companion to [[film-canon-epistemology]]'s refusal of the evaluative one.

## What it supplies

A six-channel craft taxonomy with an explicit **observable vs interpretive boundary**, a calibration reference table, and — the part the vault actually uses — a **learning order by yield per term**. Tier 1 is three terms with immediate salience in every genre: **shot scale** (Salt's seven scales), **median shot length**, and **planimetric vs recessive staging**. Each term is required to carry a physical anchor, which is what makes a canonical exemplar film function as a reference standard.

Also carries four calibration drills, of which two matter here: the staging-axis audit, and a **best-of-three Thurstone comparative heat** — comparative judgment rather than absolute scoring.

## Two independent convergences worth noting

- The report proposes a **non-compensatory gate on screenplay and structure**, arguing that a compensatory average lets strong cinematography buy back a failing script. The same object had already been derived independently from a spirits-judging nose gate before this report. Two routes, one conclusion.
- Its Thurstone heat is the same comparative machine as a spirits-judging heat.

Convergence from an independent direction is the strongest evidence either design has; note it, and do not treat the report as the origin of rules that were already in place.
