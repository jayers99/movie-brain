# How Taste Is Taught for a Living — raw/151

**"Systems-Architected Connoisseurship."** NotebookLM deep research, extracted 2026-09-11, 72 sources, ~6.8k words. The practice-first member of the taste trio — raw/149 carries the evidence, raw/150 the lineage, this one the trades that teach [[taste]] for a living.

## What it supplies

Seven forcing mechanisms, each read for what the trade compels that an ordinary encounter with the work does not.

- **The grid** (WSET's Systematic Approach, the Court of Master Sommeliers' deductive sheet) — a fixed set of discriminations in a fixed order on every sample, including the channels that look unpromising. A film review never forces an unpromising channel; a grid does. Hodgson's replicates are the counterweight: only about a tenth of elite wine judges rescored a blind triplicate within one medal class, so the grid disciplines attention without conferring reliability.
- **The firewall** (Feldman's four stages) — description, formal analysis, interpretation, judgment, with evaluative words locked out until the inventory is finished.
- **Micro-attribution** (Morelli, Berenson) — attend to the details a copyist would never think to forge, earlobes and drapery folds rather than the signature effects; on film, lens rendering and transition habits rather than the famous shot.
- **Deceleration** (Ebert's Cinema Interruptus) — any viewer calls stop and the room reads the frozen frame, inspecting cinema at the pace of a painting.
- **Interleaving** (Kornell and Bjork's painters) — shuffled artists beat blocked ones on novel attribution, while roughly four in five learners believe the reverse.
- **Contrasting cases** (Schwartz and Bransford) — one variable moves and everything else is held, 24mm against 85mm at matched head size, before any explanation is offered.
- **Comparative judgment** (Pollitt's ACJ) — pairs rather than scores, since people rate an isolated object unreliably and compare two of them consistently.

The judging pipeline is not only an analogy: blind spirits judging in heats of ten is the same family as a non-compensatory nose gate — fixed order, no absolute score, no quota.
